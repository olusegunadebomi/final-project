Admission Requirements

Review the general entry requirements before applying to an undergraduate programme at Nnamdi Azikiwe University. Requirements may vary by course and admission year.

UTME applicants

  • Choose Nnamdi Azikiwe University as your preferred institution and sit the current UTME.
  • Meet the university's current UTME score and screening requirements for your admission year.
  • Select the approved UTME subject combination for your intended programme.

O'Level qualifications

  • Hold at least five relevant credit passes, including English Language, in no more than two sittings.
  • Some programmes require Mathematics or other specific subjects; check the course requirements before applying.
  • Upload and verify your results through the official admissions process.

Direct Entry applicants

  • Apply through the current JAMB Direct Entry process and satisfy the general O'Level requirements.
  • Provide an accepted advanced qualification relevant to the chosen programme.
  • Meet any additional faculty or departmental conditions for the intended level of entry.

Screening and verification

  • Complete the university's post-UTME or Direct Entry screening when announced.
  • Use accurate personal and academic details that match your official records.
  • Follow published deadlines and keep copies of submitted documents and confirmation details.

Before you apply: score thresholds, accepted qualifications, subject combinations and screening procedures can change each session. Confirm the current details on the official Nnamdi Azikiwe University website and the relevant JAMB admissions portal.
